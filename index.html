jsl@szu.edu.cn Google Scholar GitHub ORCID DBLP Semantic Scholar

I work on trustworthy machine learning and computer vision, with a focus on learning from limited or imperfect supervision and making models reliable under uncertainty, occlusion, and distribution shift.

I received my Ph.D. from Central South University in 2024 and subsequently worked there as a postdoctoral researcher. I was also a visiting Ph.D. student at Peng Cheng Laboratory.

Research interests

  • Trustworthy learning. Semi-supervised learning, pseudo-label selection, and uncertainty estimation.
  • Visual perception. Image, video, and 3D segmentation; occlusion and incomplete observations.
  • Multimodal understanding. Representation learning across vision, language, and structured information.
  • Structured modeling. Connecting visual perception with mechanism-based models for complex systems.

Current focus (2024–present): Domain generalization & test-time adaptation; learning with noisy labels & knowledge distillation; LLM hallucination detection.
